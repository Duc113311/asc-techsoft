Privacy Policy
Water Nudge

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Water Nudge ("we", "our", or "the app") is a hydration tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, collected, used, and handled when you use Water Nudge.

By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect and Use

Water Nudge is designed to keep your personal hydration information locally on your device.

Data stored locally on your device

  • Profile information such as gender, age, weight, and height
  • Wake-up and bedtime settings
  • Daily hydration goal
  • Preferred measurement units
  • Drink and hydration records
  • Daily hydration summaries
  • Reminder schedules
  • Application preferences, including theme and language

This information is used to provide hydration tracking, reminders, and personalization. Water Nudge does not transmit this locally stored data to our servers.

Third-Party Services

Water Nudge uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain device, application, diagnostic, or usage information:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Remote Config

These services may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Device model and operating system information
  • Application version and configuration
  • App interaction and usage events
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • IP address

Health Connect (Android)

Water Nudge provides an optional integration with Health Connect by Android. This integration is disabled by default and becomes active only when you enable "Health Connect" in the app settings and explicitly grant the requested permission.

Data written

When enabled, Water Nudge writes hydration volume and date/time of drinks you record in the app to Health Connect.

How data is used

Health Connect data is used solely to sync hydration records you create in Water Nudge. Processing occurs on your device. We do not upload Health Connect data to our servers or any third parties.

Your control

You can grant, deny, or revoke the Health Connect permission at any time through Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Water Nudge.

Water Nudge's access to and use of Health Connect data complies with applicable Google Play Health Connect requirements, including its Limited Use requirements.

Notifications

Water Nudge may send local notifications to provide hydration reminders and daily hydration progress updates. You can disable notification permissions at any time through Android system settings.

Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, Water Nudge may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to send hydration reminders
  • Health Connect (write hydration) — only if you enable the Health Connect sync feature

The app requests permissions only when required for the corresponding user-facing functionality.

Service Providers

We may use third-party companies or services to provide application infrastructure, deliver notifications, analyze application performance, and diagnose crashes. These service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own privacy policies and legal obligations.

Data Retention

Data stored by Water Nudge

Hydration records, profile information, preferences, and other locally stored app data remain on your device until you delete the relevant data within the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Third-party data

Information processed by Firebase or Google Play Services is retained according to each provider's applicable retention settings and privacy policies.

How to Delete Your Data

  • Delete Water Nudge data: Android Settings → Apps → Water Nudge → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the app. This action cannot be undone.
  • Delete Health Connect data written by Water Nudge: Android Settings → Health Connect → Data and access → Hydration.
  • Revoke Health Connect permission: Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Water Nudge.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information associated with the use of Water Nudge. However, no electronic storage system or method of data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Health Connect data is processed only for the hydration sync functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Water Nudge is not directed toward children under the minimum age required to independently consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Links to Other Websites

Water Nudge may contain links to third-party websites or services. We do not operate these external services and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party website or service you visit.
